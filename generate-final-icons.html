📋 Installation Instructions

  1. Download: Click the big button above to download all 3 icon files
  2. Find Files: Check your Downloads folder on your Mac
  3. Replace Icons: Copy these 3 files to your project's /public folder:
    • apple-touch-icon.png ← Most important for iOS!
    • icon-192.png
    • icon-512.png
  4. Rebuild: Run npm run build in your terminal
  5. Clear Cache: On your Mac, press Cmd+Shift+R in browser
  6. iPhone Test:
    • Delete the app from your iPhone home screen
    • Clear Safari cache (Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data)
    • Reopen the app in Safari and add to home screen again
  7. Verify: The birdie should now be perfectly centered with no cropping! 🎉
