🏌️ Birdies Icon Generator

Create perfectly padded app icons that won't get cropped on iOS home screens

⚠️ iOS Icon Cropping Issue:
iOS applies an automatic mask to all app icons, which can crop the edges. Use 35-40% padding to ensure nothing gets cut off.

📱 Recommended: 35% for iOS (prevents edge cropping)
🔧 If edges still appear cropped, try 40%

📋 Instructions:
  1. Adjust padding slider until the preview looks good (default 35% works well)
  2. Click individual download buttons OR "Download All Sizes" button
  3. Save files to your /public folder
  4. Update your manifest.json and index.html to use these new icons
  5. Clear app cache and reinstall PWA to see changes
